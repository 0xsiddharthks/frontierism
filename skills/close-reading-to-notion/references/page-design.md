# Layered study page

Begin the body with **Executive summary**, without repeating its page title or displaying a large metadata table. State the problem, proposed answer, operative mechanism, and principal tension in a short paragraph or a few purposeful bullets. Follow it with the link to the full original text and a compact author/edition line when useful. Native properties hold filterable metadata.

The next layer explains the core question and argument. A productive shape is:

1. **The question and stakes:** what decision is at issue, against which alternative, for whom?
2. **Understand it from first principles:** explain necessary terms and walk through the causal or institutional mechanism. Include a concrete example if useful.
3. **How the argument works:** a source-located sequence of premises and inferences, including the hardest step and the evidence offered.
4. **The crux and strongest objection:** the decisive assumption, the best challenge, the author's reply where present, and your residual assessment.
5. **Why it matters:** specific political, social, or economic implications and a clearly marked modern analogy if warranted.
6. **Connections and recall:** a small number of explained links and a few questions, with folded answer sketches.

Adapt or combine sections when that reads better. A short essay does not need a long apparatus; a complicated argument deserves more than a compressed inventory. Avoid generic “this remains relevant today” endings. Distinguish source claims and your analysis through explicit attribution and local labels, not a disclaimer that the reader must remember from another page.

Use source sections and short passage-opening phrases to locate the reasoning. If using extraction block IDs, label them as analyst locators and explain their exact edition/hash dependency in the collection guide. Do not invent web fragments from those IDs. An original's editorial title, the analyst's study title, and a series ordinal are different fields.

Keep user-owned notes in an identifiable section. Use native Notion mentions for internal navigation. Previous/next links follow the stable manifest, not whichever pages finished publication first. Add no predecessor to the first text and no successor to the last.
